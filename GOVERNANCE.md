# Governance

Maithic is maintained by the Maithic Authors. Maintainers review and merge contributions, cut releases and are
responsible for the security policy and the code of conduct.

- Decisions are made in the open, on issues and pull requests, by consensus of the maintainers.
- A decision that shapes the design is recorded in [`docs/adr/`](docs/adr) with its context and consequences.
- A change to what counts as `verified`, to the default trust policy or to the handling of keys needs the
  agreement of two maintainers and an entry in the change log.
- Anyone who has made sustained, good contributions may be invited to become a maintainer.
