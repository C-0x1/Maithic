# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a place where people of every background and level of experience can work on
mathematics and software together.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient. Assume good intentions and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Criticize ideas, proofs and code, not people.
- Give credit, and accept correction gracefully.
- Keep discussions on topic and welcoming to newcomers.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks, sexualized language or imagery, publishing private information
without permission, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainers at the address in [`SUPPORT.md`](SUPPORT.md). Reports are kept confidential.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may
temporarily or permanently exclude a contributor after a warning, or without one in serious cases.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.
